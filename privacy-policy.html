Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This policy explains what AffiMix ("we," "us," "the site") collects when you visit, and how it's used. AffiMix is a small, independently run site — there's no ad network selling your data behind the scenes here.

What we collect

  • Feedback submissions: if you use the feedback bar, your rating and any note you type is sent by opening your device's email app, addressed to us. Nothing is stored on the site itself — the submission only exists once it lands in our inbox.
  • Basic visit data: our hosting provider and any analytics tool we enable may log standard technical data such as approximate location, browser type, device type, and pages viewed. This is aggregate and not used to identify you personally.
  • Affiliate link clicks: when you click an affiliate link, the destination retailer's own tracking takes over once you leave this site. We don't see your purchase details — only that a click happened, if the retailer's program reports it back.

Cookies

AffiMix does not set its own tracking cookies. If an affiliate network or analytics tool is added later, this section will be updated to name it specifically and explain what it does.

How we use what's collected

  • To read and respond to feedback
  • To understand which pages and picks are getting attention, so the content stays useful
  • To keep the site running reliably and securely

What we don't do

We don't sell visitor data. We don't share your feedback submissions with third parties beyond what's needed to receive the email itself.

Third-party links

Affiliate links and the Linktree page take you to sites we don't control. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave AffiMix — worth a glance if you're unsure.

Children's privacy

AffiMix isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can browse AffiMix without submitting any feedback at all, and nothing personal is required to view picks or use the Linktree link. To ask what's on file about you or to request removal of a feedback submission, email us directly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date at the top will reflect it.
