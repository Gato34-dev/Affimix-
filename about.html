About

One person, two obsessions, cross-checked.

AffiMix started as a running note comparing tech gadgets against actual health outcomes — sleep, focus, recovery — instead of just specs. It grew into a public list.

How picks get chosen

Every product on AffiMix is judged on the same two questions: does it do what it claims, and is it worth the price compared to the obvious alternatives. Health items are weighed against actual use over time, not just first impressions; tech items are weighed against build quality and whether the "smart" features are actually useful day to day.

Who's behind it

AffiMix is run independently, not by a brand or agency. Questions, corrections, or pushback on a pick are welcome — reach out any time.

How the site earns money

Some links on AffiMix are affiliate links, meaning a purchase through them can earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes which products get featured — see the full Affiliate Disclosure for specifics.
